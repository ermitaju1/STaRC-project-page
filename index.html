Abstract

Why saliency?

Dense video captioning requires both accurate event localization and faithful caption generation. STaRC treats saliency as a reusable temporal supervision signal: the same saliency distribution guides segmentation, retrieval, and generation, reducing inconsistency between modules that otherwise rely on separate temporal cues.

Method

Unified saliency-based design

01

Supervised Saliency Training

Learn a saliency distribution from annotation-derived temporal supervision so highlight frames compete for probability mass.

02

Saliency-Guided Segmentation

Use saliency to generate event-like segments that better align with ground-truth event boundaries.

03

Saliency-Aware Retrieval

Retrieve captions or features around salient temporal regions to provide event-level context to the generator.

04

Decoder Conditioning

Feed saliency-consistent signals into the captioning decoder, aligning what the model segments, retrieves, and describes.

Key Insight

One temporal signal, multiple roles

Instead of treating segmentation, retrieval, and generation as loosely connected stages, STaRC makes saliency the common currency across the pipeline. Better temporal segments provide cleaner retrieval context, and cleaner retrieval context supports stronger dense captions.
